The Spirit of Utsuwa

At Gallery AEHI, we curate exceptional pottery from Japanese artisans who dedicate their lives to the craft. Each piece, or "Utsuwa," is more than just a vessel; it is a blend of traditional techniques and modern sensibilities.

Our mission is to bridge the silent dialogue between the creator and the soul of your home, bringing the timeless beauty of Japanese ceramic art to dining tables worldwide.

Before You Buy

Every piece at Gallery AEHI is handmade. Subtle variations in color, size, and texture are testament to the artisan's hand and the kiln's fire. We invite you to embrace these unique characteristics as part of the beauty of Japanese pottery.

Shipping & Delivery

We ship internationally from Japan. Each item is carefully packaged in traditional wooden boxes or protective wrapping to ensure its safe arrival at your doorstep.
